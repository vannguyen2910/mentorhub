# Tasty Route (fictional): public page

**Price:** delivery fee from $1.80. No service fee.

**Delivery:** choose as soon as possible, or schedule up to 7 days ahead. You can change the slot until 30 minutes before delivery. If you are not home, choose "leave at the door".

**Cancel or edit:** cancel with one tap on the home screen until the restaurant accepts. Edit the address until the courier is assigned.

**First order:** a three-question quiz at sign-up (diet, budget, area). A welcome voucher.

**Delivery estimates:** live courier position, with the estimate updated every minute.
