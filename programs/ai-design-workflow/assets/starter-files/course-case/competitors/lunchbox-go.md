# Lunchbox Go (fictional): public page

**Price:** free delivery on orders over $10, otherwise $2.50.

**Delivery:** as soon as possible, or one fixed lunch slot (12:00 to 13:00). You can change the slot until 11:00.

**Cancel or edit:** manage the order in "Order details".

**First order:** no onboarding. You go straight to the restaurants after sign-up.

**Delivery estimates:** "Usually under 30 minutes."
